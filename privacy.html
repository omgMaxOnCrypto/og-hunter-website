DRAFT – This document is under preparation and is not a finalized privacy notice. Account integrations are not yet publicly available.

1. About OG HUNTER

OG HUNTER is a username management project with a Telegram bot and a planned web dashboard. Its features include username idea generation, saved collections and optional account analytics.

2. Data Processing

The project currently uses a local SQLite database to store saved usernames and manually recorded availability notes. The Telegram bot processes commands and messages needed to respond to users.

Additional processing, retention periods, hosting arrangements and legal bases must be documented before public operation.

3. TikTok and Instagram Connections

Future integrations may allow users to authorize access to supported profile statistics through official platform APIs.

The exact permissions, token storage methods, retention periods and account disconnection procedures will be documented before launch.

4. Third-Party Services

The project uses or plans to use services including GitHub Pages, Telegram and official platform APIs. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

5. Your Rights and Contact

Information about the responsible operator, applicable privacy rights and a working contact method must be added before public launch.
